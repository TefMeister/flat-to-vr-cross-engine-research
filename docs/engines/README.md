# Per-engine family pages

One page per engine family this account has at least one conversion project on. Each page is the
**router** for its family: the engine's identity, the known public VR path, the shared truths our
sibling projects on that engine have proved, and links out to every project's `ENGINE-DOSSIER.md`
for the game-specific detail.

The per-game dossiers stay in their own `-engine-research` repos and never move or merge — these
pages are a *view* over them, not a relocation. They are curated by the cross-project research
sweep, which reads every project's research and folds anything that holds across a family into
the family's page.
