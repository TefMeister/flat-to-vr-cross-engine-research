# fholger's OpenXR-Simulator: an MIT, open-source desktop OpenXR runtime for headset-free testing

**From:** `/gr` estate sweep, home PC, 2026-09-17.
**Related:** the library's MGS5VR case study (item 12, Meta XR Simulator).

**OpenXR-Simulator** by **fholger** (author of Crysis VR / Far Cry VR) is a lightweight OpenXR runtime
that runs VR apps in a desktop window with side-by-side stereo, mouse-and-keyboard pose control,
D3D11, D3D12 and OpenGL backends, and PowerShell register/unregister scripts; tested with Unity, Unreal
(via UEVR) and the Steam overlay `[reported 2026-09-17, from its README]`. MIT licence.

Why it matters estate-wide: the dev PC has no headset. Meta's XR Simulator is closed and tied to Meta's
stack; this is open, small, and fits an OpenXR-output mod on the dev PC `[hypothesis]`.

Source: <https://github.com/fholger/OpenXR-Simulator>
