## Identity

- **Engine:** Criterion Software's RenderWare — the widely licensed 2000s middleware engine
  (used across many publishers' titles of the era).
- **Render API:** Direct3D 8/9 era; see the dossier for what the Manhunt PC build actually uses.
- **Known public VR path:** none turnkey. Manual build; for anything Direct3D 8 or older, a
  D3D8-to-modern wrapper comes first (see the engines index's last row).
