# Contributing

Anyone is welcome to add to this library. It exists so the public knowledge on modding flat
games into VR lives in one readable, well-attributed place. Contributions are held to three
simple, non-negotiable rules.

## The three rules

1. **Public information only.** Everything you add must already be publicly available — a public
   repo, a docs site, a forum/Discord/Reddit post, an article, a video, a released tool. Do not
   add private communications, leaked material, or anything behind an NDA. Do not add
   copyrighted engine source or game assets.
2. **Link the primary source for every claim.** Say where each fact, technique, or figure comes
   from and link it. Describe mechanisms in your own words and quote only **short** excerpts
   (enough to be useful, not a copy of someone's file). If a technique comes from a specific
   repo/commit/doc, link that exact place.
3. **Credit everyone.** Add each new person, project, or tool you drew on to
   [`ATTRIBUTION.md`](./ATTRIBUTION.md), with a link and — where relevant — their license. If in
   doubt, over-credit.

## What belongs here

- Engine-agnostic techniques and patterns (hooks, stereo, timing, projection, HUD, input).
- Worked case studies of engines someone has already brought into VR **in public**, described
  as study notes with links — not copied source trees.
- Landscape/orientation material: which public tool fits which engine, and why.
- Corrections and updates to anything already here.

## What does not belong here

- Whole copies of other projects' source files (link to them instead).
- Game assets, copyrighted engine source, or any non-public material.
- Circumvention guidance aimed at DRM/anti-cheat for its own sake — this library documents
  rendering/VR techniques for legitimately-owned games, not piracy or cheating.

## How to contribute

Open a pull request or an issue. Keep docs in Markdown, match the existing structure, and update
`ATTRIBUTION.md` in the same PR. By contributing you agree your original writing is licensed
under this repo's [CC-BY-4.0](./LICENSE); any third-party material you reference stays under its
own license.

## Corrections & removals

If you are credited or referenced here — as an author, contributor, or source — and want your
name or information changed or removed, email **td3kxlvr@proton.me** and tell us **exactly what to
remove**. We will do it promptly, no problem and no argument; you do not need to prove ownership or
explain why. Likewise, if you should be credited and aren't, tell us and we'll fix it. And to be
clear: we document techniques and link to sources — **we do not copy or reuse anyone's actual code
or files**, regardless of license or price.
